## What we are exploring

Our research follows AI4S → RSI → AI4S: use artificial intelligence to advance scientific research, improve intelligent systems through that research, and apply more capable intelligence to further exploration, so scientific discovery and recursive self-improvement reinforce each other.

Financial markets are our starting point. In a highly non-stationary, adversarial, and uncertain real-world environment, our research goes beyond “better predictive models” to ask: How can agents maintain reliable decision-making as conditions change? How can strategies be generated and validated, then compete and continually evolve through multi-agent collaboration and strategic interaction? How can a system improve the methods that produce strategies, rather than merely optimize an individual strategy?

We hope to move gradually from “humans design strategies, machines execute them” to a collaborative research model in which “humans define problems, objectives, and boundaries, while systems autonomously propose hypotheses, run experiments, validate, and iterate on strategies.” Greater autonomy must be matched by stronger independent validation, adversarial testing and defenses for AI, traceable decision-making, and risk controls.

We are also exploring organizational practices that align with this technical direction. Through open problems, modular collaboration, contribution records, value assessment, and explainable distribution mechanisms with avenues for appeal, we want those who ask critical questions, build shared infrastructure, find errors, validate hypotheses, and create long-term value to have the opportunity to receive rewards commensurate with their contributions. Technology can expand what we create together; fair distribution requires careful design.

Starting with intelligent financial decision-making, our long-term interests include scientific research, humans and AI learning together, and the possibilities at the intersection of artificial intelligence and neuroscience. We remain committed to problems worth solving while retaining the ability to change methods, revise our judgments, and start again.

Keeping agents running reliably is an infrastructure capability; enabling them to discover new knowledge more reliably is a research capability; enabling them to continually improve themselves within verifiable, controllable boundaries is the evolutionary capability we are exploring.

## S2E distributed AI collaboration ecosystem

S2E (Singularity to Evolution) is a distributed AI collaboration ecosystem under development, connecting researchers, domain experts, engineers, AI agents, and computing resources worldwide to organize sustained collaboration on difficult problems shared by humanity.

We focus on three long-term questions:

Energy and habitats: can humanity obtain more abundant energy and extend sustainable habitats beyond Earth?

Life and intelligence: can humanity reduce the limitations imposed by disease and aging, and deepen its understanding of life, cognition, and consciousness?

Collaboration and civilization: can people, agents, knowledge, and resources distributed worldwide develop a sustained capacity to solve complex problems together?

We will explore how to break difficult problems and ambitious challenges into verifiable research tasks, starting with specific questions and tackling them step by step. Fusion research can begin with a control or materials problem; brain–computer interfaces with cross-person decoding and long-term stability; space-based computing with thermal management, radiation, and cost models. Ambitious questions provide a shared direction; small, verifiable tasks enable participants to begin collaborating.

The platform encourages participants to capture questions, methods, and research records during their most creative hours, with agents collaborating asynchronously within authorized boundaries to reduce repeated communication and waiting. We are exploring progressive trust and controlled sharing, using blockchain to support contribution traceability and rewarding genuine contributions through attribution, reputation, research funding, and opportunities to use resources.

S2E is using real tasks in the financial sector to test mechanisms for collaboration, validation, and reward distribution, while also developing infrastructure, connecting partners across disciplines, and building the ecosystem. The platform is committed to the public benefit of scientific research and community collaboration, iterates quickly with a small core team, and welcomes researchers, developers, research institutions, and resource partners worldwide.

Let one person's important discovery become the starting point for others to explore further.

The following solution-submission process applies only to `SX-CH-001` through `SX-CH-004`. The [financial research question program](open-research/README.md) accepts unresolved questions with prior thinking or investigation and does not use the work-delivery requirements below.

1. Read the [challenge board](challenge-board/README.md), the selected challenge, and the [latest grade status](challenge-board/STATUS.md).
2. Prepare a complete code package, running instructions, reproduction commands, test results, environment details, known limitations, and rights statements.
3. Submit privately by default to `join@singularityx.tech` with the subject: `[Challenge Submission] <Challenge ID> - <Name>`. The email body must include **your real name, valid contact details, and the full URL of your own personal GitHub profile** (`https://github.com/<username>`). Provide at least one regularly checked email address, phone number, or WeChat ID. The profile URL is mandatory; a username alone, repository URL, organization profile, or another person's profile is insufficient.
4. Use a public Pull Request only when intentionally disclosing the code and report. You must still email all three required details to the address above and include the PR URL. Never publish private contact details, credentials, private data, account information, or non-public strategies.

**Name, valid contact details, and a personal GitHub profile URL are mandatory for both initial and upgraded submissions. Missing information, invalid contact details, or a non-compliant profile URL makes the submission incomplete; formal review will not begin until the information is complete.** Use the [submission email template](docs/challenge-submission-template.md).

Each contributor has one initial submission opportunity per challenge. While Grade A remains open, an initial submission that does not meet Grade S but does meet Grade A may be formally certified at Grade A. That certification unlocks one complete Grade S-only upgrade within five calendar days (120 hours) from the time the Grade A confirmation email is sent, for a maximum of two submissions per contributor per challenge. After Grade A is announced closed, later submissions are evaluated only against grades that remain open and receive no supplemental Grade A decision or equivalent review. For a challenge where only Grade S remains open, an initial submission that does not meet Grade S does not create a second submission opportunity. See the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md), the selected challenge, and the [latest grade status](challenge-board/STATUS.md) for complete rules.

## Value assessment and fair distribution

We want long-term contribution inside the organization to be grounded in verifiable evidence and actual adoption—not in title, presentation skills, hours worked, or a single outcome. Research, data, engineering, validation, risk control, documentation, shared tools, and collaboration support can all constitute valid contribution. Assessment considers both what an individual delivered and how different parts of the team combined to produce the result.

### What value assessment considers

| Dimension | What we examine |
|---|---|
| Validity and adoption | Whether the contribution addresses a real task, is adopted, and produces a verifiable result. |
| Quality and trust | Whether the result is reproducible, evidence is complete, the method is robust, and limitations are clearly disclosed. |
| Marginal impact | How much value the work adds—or how much risk or uncertainty it removes—compared with the counterfactual without it. |
| Long-term impact | Whether the result can be reused, maintained, extended, and retained as an organizational asset. |
| Risk and collaboration | Whether the work respects permission, compliance, and risk discipline and improves whole-team delivery. |

Assessment follows a contribution-record, evidence-verification, value-attribution, and human-review process. Self-reported claims may provide leads but do not receive full weight by themselves; material conclusions require support from code, data, experiments, review records, or evidence of actual adoption. Where appropriate, ablation, counterfactual analysis, walk-forward validation, and controlled comparisons may support marginal-impact assessment, but algorithmic output remains an input to review rather than a final decision.

### How fair distribution works

- **Define the distribution boundary first:** formal agreements specify the value pool, eligible participants, settlement conditions, and exclusions. Public-challenge bounties and long-term member contribution are administered separately.
- **Use multiple contribution anchors:** direct delivery, marginal impact, counterfactual necessity, long-term reuse, risk control, and collaboration are considered together; hours, title, or one score do not determine the result by themselves.
- **Recognize shared outcomes:** shared tools, infrastructure, validation work, and team collaboration should receive appropriate recognition rather than assigning all value to the last visible submitter.
- **Preserve the total:** distribution cannot exceed the confirmed value pool, the same outcome cannot be counted twice, and uncertain or immature value may remain deferred.
- **Keep review and appeal available:** material adjustments require recorded reasons and conflict disclosure, and affected members may request review within the applicable window.

The purpose is not to replace judgment with one score, but to make judgment evidence-based, distribution bounded, adjustments recorded, and outcomes explainable.

**Detailed framework:** [Value Assessment System](docs/value-assessment-system.md) | [Value Distribution Protocol](VALUE-DISTRIBUTION-PROTOCOL.md)

All compensation, bounties, profit sharing, equity, options, tokens, points, or other economic interests are governed by formal agreements, applicable law, tax requirements, and organizational approval.
