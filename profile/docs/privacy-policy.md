# SingularityX Privacy Policy

[中文版本](privacy-policy.zh-CN.md)

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to the technical-bounty and builder-collaboration services provided by SingularityX through its public code repository, official email, and related challenge-review processes. Third-party websites and services, including GitHub, process information under their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on how you participate, we may process:

- your name, GitHub username, email address, location, and other contact details;
- résumés, project history, public work, and membership-application materials;
- challenge IDs, code packages, documents, logs, test results, file hashes, submission times, and version records;
- statements about originality, authorization, open-source licenses, data sources, and compliance;
- review communications, reproduction records, conclusions, appeals, and dispute materials;
- information needed to verify award recipients and process bounty payment, invoicing, or tax matters; and
- technical records reasonably necessary for security, anti-cheating, troubleshooting, and compliance retention.

Do not submit unrelated personal information, account credentials, private keys, customer records, private trading records, restricted data, or information you are not authorized to disclose.

## 3. Purposes of Processing

We use information only as reasonably necessary to receive and manage submissions, communicate with participants, conduct technical review and reproduction, verify rights and compliance, prevent cheating and security risks, publish anonymized results, process bounties and taxes, address appeals and complaints, and meet legal obligations.

We process information based on consent, necessity to enter into or perform applicable rules or agreements, necessity to meet legal obligations, or another basis allowed by applicable law. If the purpose, method, or categories of information materially change, we provide a new notice and obtain consent where required.

Identity, financial-account, tax, or personal information of a child under 14 may be sensitive personal information. We process it only for a specific purpose and where strictly necessary, provide any additional notice required by law, apply stricter safeguards, and obtain separate consent where required. Do not provide this information through a public channel.

Technical tools may assist testing, anti-cheating, and review, but decisions with a material effect on eligibility, grades, or bounties are not made solely by automated means and require human review.

## 4. Public Display

A private submission is not automatically made public. We publish reviewed materials only with authorization, where task rules expressly allow it, or where required by law, and obtain separate consent where legally required. Grade-recognized work is generally displayed only through an anonymous ID, grade, bounty status, and anonymized summary. Authorized profiles or code materials are displayed for one week under the current challenge rules and then their public entry points are removed.

Content placed in a public Pull Request, Issue, or other public channel can be viewed by anyone. Contributors must remove sensitive information before posting publicly.

## 5. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell personal information. We may provide the minimum necessary information to service providers, professional advisers, or competent authorities for code hosting, email, cloud storage, review, identity verification, payment, tax, legal, or security purposes and require processing consistent with applicable obligations. We provide additional recipient information or obtain separate consent where the law requires it. If the organization is restructured, merged, or transfers assets, we provide legally required notice about the recipient and require continued protection under this policy; a change in purpose or method requires renewed consent where applicable.

## 6. International Processing

GitHub, email, cloud services, or participants may be located in different countries or regions, so information may be processed outside a participant’s location. If this constitutes a cross-border transfer of personal information, we provide any legally required notice about the overseas recipient, purpose, method, information categories, and exercise of rights; obtain separate consent where required; and complete any applicable security assessment, certification, standard contract, or other procedure. Contributors must also ensure they are authorized to make any relevant cross-border transfer and must not submit data restricted by location or contract.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

We retain information for the shortest period reasonably necessary for review, re-check, settlement, anti-cheating, security audits, disputes, finance and tax, and legal requirements. Where a specific period can be determined, it is stated in task rules, settlement documents, or a separate notice. Otherwise, we consider whether the purpose is complete, appeal or dispute periods have ended, and financial, tax, and legal retention duties have expired. We then delete, anonymize, or restrict processing where reasonably practicable. Deletion from backups, audit records, or legally required archives may occur later.

## 8. Security

We use access controls, least privilege, backups, audit practices, and human review appropriate to the nature and risk of the information. No system can guarantee absolute security. If information is or may be disclosed, altered, or lost, we take legally required remedial action and notify affected individuals and authorities where applicable. If you discover a suspected disclosure or accidental submission, contact us promptly at the address below.

## 9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, copies, correction, supplementation, deletion, transfer, or restriction of personal information; withdraw consent-based authorization; object to processing; or request an explanation of the processing rules. We may verify identity to protect information. Information required for review, settlement, disputes, compliance, or legal retention may not be deleted immediately. If we deny a request, we provide the reason where required; you may complain to a competent authority or seek other lawful remedies.

## 10. Minors

The services are not primarily directed to minors. A child under 14 must not submit personal information independently; where participation is necessary, a parent or other guardian must contact us and provide consent in advance. Other minors should participate only with a guardian’s consent and guidance and should avoid submitting unnecessary identity information.

## 11. Updates and Contact

We may update this policy when services, rules, or legal requirements change and will publish the revised date in the repository or relevant page. Send privacy requests, data-security reports, or complaints to `join@singularityx.tech`.
