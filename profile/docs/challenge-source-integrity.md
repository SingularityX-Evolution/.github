# Challenge Source Integrity

**Rule interpretation body:** SingularityX

The public challenge pages in this package are GitHub-rendered Markdown pages prepared for external publication. They preserve the public background, core problem, public task scope, submission-opportunity and grade-closure rules, deliverable standards, recognition standards, S / A bounty tiers, and rule-interpretation body while excluding private evaluation data and internal review records. SX-CH-004 also publishes a separate linked evaluation standard for its V2.0 public-data research protocol.

Challenge pages are ordered by CNY bounty cap from low to high, and challenge IDs follow the same amount-ascending order. The 2026-10-20 appeal-rule update synchronizes the four task pages and the SX-CH-004 evaluation standard: a 5-working-day appeal window after receipt of results, a reply within 3 working days after receipt of an appeal, and no second-submission count for appeals or correction of reviewer errors. The subsequent same-day clarification adds only supplementary-material boundaries under Section 5.8 of the common rules. The hashes below also reflect the requirement to include progress and arrangements for the next update in the timely reply when reconsideration is incomplete.
