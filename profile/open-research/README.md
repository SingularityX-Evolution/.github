# Financial Research Question Proposals and Adoption Awards

[中文](README.zh-CN.md) | [Platform home](../README.md) | [Adopted questions and challenge records](STATUS.md) | [Question template](templates/question.md)

**All rights of interpretation are reserved by SingularityX Evolution (零界演化).**

SingularityX welcomes unresolved financial and quantitative research questions that individuals, teams or organizations have already thought through or investigated. Participation requires no internal affiliation or invitation, has no registration fee, and remains open without a common deadline.

**At this stage, only question proposals are accepted, not already solved problems or complete solutions. When a question is adopted, the proposer receives a question award and the question is developed into a public challenge inviting further research.**

## 1. Questions eligible for submission

A proposal should meet all of the following conditions:

1. It concerns financial and quantitative research and identifies a specific setting, scope and research value.
2. The proposer has undertaken actual thinking or investigation and can explain possible explanations considered, materials reviewed, methods attempted or difficulties encountered.
3. The core question remains unresolved, clearly distinguished from existing knowledge and interim findings.
4. Further research is possible, with an objective that can be developed into a public challenge.

Code, experiments and successful outcomes are not prerequisites. Specific reasoning, literature analysis, method comparisons, practical observations and failure records can support a proposal. Identify sources and conditions for any cited material, data or experiments. Broad themes, undeveloped ideas or proposals without any account of prior thinking or investigation require further material.

Already solved problems, complete solutions offered for display or transfer, and applications for solution, improvement or independent-verification awards fall outside this stage. Partial findings may provide context, but the submitted objective must be a specific, genuinely unresolved question. Completed work cannot replace a question proposal.

## 2. Research scope

The initial scope is financial and quantitative research along `AI4S → RSI → AI4S`: AI advances research, research improves intelligent systems, and improved systems support further discovery.

| Direction | Question areas |
|---|---|
| Financial mechanisms and hypotheses | Explanations of market phenomena, limitations of existing methods and distinguishing competing hypotheses |
| Expert logic slices and capsules | Applicability, failure boundaries, labels and reuse of context-specific decision logic |
| Multi-agent systems and strategy scheduling | Unresolved issues in collaboration, strategic interaction, strategy generation, competition, combination and suspension |
| Research method improvement | Specific bottlenecks in experiment selection, training or research workflows |
| Reliability and risk control | Research questions concerning data leakage, backtest bias, costs, robustness, and AI red teaming and defense |

AI or agents may assist thinking and investigation, with material use disclosed and an identified person or organization responsible for the submission. Expert reputation, isolated successes and AI-generated conclusions do not replace analysis. Participants need not provide live trading accounts or invest trading capital.

## 3. How to submit

Use the [question template](templates/question.md) and email [join@singularityx.tech](mailto:join@singularityx.tech?subject=%5BOpen%20Question%5D) with subject `[Open Question] <Question title>`.

Include:

- **Question and value:** financial setting, scope, limitations of existing methods and reasons the problem matters.
- **Prior thinking or effort:** explanations actually considered, sources reviewed, analysis, attempted methods or experiments, distinguishing completed work from plans.
- **Remaining difficulty:** interim understanding, evidence, inferences, untested claims and the unresolved core objective.
- **Further research suggestions:** what challengers should investigate, with any initial validation ideas, resources or constraints. A complete acceptance specification is not required from the proposer.
- **Contact and disclosure:** name or entity, valid contact details, sources, permitted public materials and attribution preference.

Email submissions are private by default. Suitable material that you are entitled to publish may use the repository Issues form “公开研究提问 / Research question”; then email the Issue URL and contact details. Issues and attachments are public. Do not include private contact information, payment details or confidential materials in them.

Code is optional. If exploratory code is attached, privately provide the responsible person's real name, valid contact details and full personal GitHub profile URL; list the repository separately. Such attachments evidence prior investigation, not an application for a solution award at this stage.

## 4. Review, adoption and awards

The platform uses the question description, prior thinking or investigation and unresolved difficulty to assess research value and suitability for a public challenge.

**Question awards are determined by the question’s research significance, practical value, importance, potential reach and value as a public challenge, not by solution progress or the completeness of results.** Prior thinking or investigation is a submission requirement that explains the basis of the question and its unresolved difficulty; time spent, code volume, experiment counts and the amount of completed work do not determine the award tier.

| Question award, CNY before tax | Question-value criteria |
|---|---|
| 2,000 | A question with clear financial research or practical value, addressing a specific limitation and suitable for a well-defined public challenge |
| 5,000 | A question addressing a representative or widespread bottleneck, with substantial potential to improve research methods, system capabilities or practical applications |
| 10,000 | A question of exceptional research or practical importance, concerning a foundational, shared or frontier problem that can advance related research and methods |

For a proposed adoption, the platform confirms the question, tier, contributors, attribution and disclosure scope with the proposer, then issues a written adoption notice. **The formal adoption notice confirms the question award. Receipt, requests for additional material, initial opinions and a participant-created Issue are not adoption.**

Following adoption, the confirmed award is paid and the question becomes a public challenge. No responses, unsuccessful solutions, changes to publication arrangements or a still-unresolved question do not cancel an established question award. If the platform actually publishes a submitted question as a formal challenge, omission of an adoption notice must not be used to avoid the established award obligation.

Each question initially receives one tier; sending the same material again does not earn another payment. Similar proposals are assessed by specific objectives, independent thinking and additional investigation, not titles alone. Joint contributions and allocation are confirmed before adoption. Rewording, splitting or combining a statement must preserve contribution records.

Non-adopted questions may be supplemented, and participants may propose other unresolved questions without a general submission-count limit. Only a new substantive question contribution can receive a separate assessment; rephrasing the same content does not create another award.

## 5. Feedback, payment and challenge publication

Working days follow the mainland China statutory calendar, starting on the next working day after the relevant event. Completeness is measured by actual receipt of required material, not delayed acknowledgment.

| Stage | Time and feedback |
|---|---|
| Receipt and completeness | Acknowledge receipt, check completeness and identify recognizable missing items together |
| Initial question review | Within 5 working days of complete material, provide a proposed adoption decision, required additions or reasons for non-adoption |
| Formal adoption | Issue the written notice after adoption conditions are met and the question, award and publication permissions are confirmed |
| Award payment | Process after formal adoption and verification of the required payment and tax information; communicate payment arrangements by email and explain applicable tax withholding |
| Public challenge | After formal adoption, publish a challenge page or announcement with an identifier, objectives, solution conditions and submission channel |
| Reconsideration | Respond within 3 working days of complete material; complex cases receive findings to date and further handling arrangements |

Where additional material or disclosure agreement is needed, specify pending items and normally report progress every 5 working days. If complexity or further checks require an extension to review or reconsideration, explain the reason and next feedback point before the original deadline. Text clarifications do not restart the entire review; substantive additions restart only the affected assessment, with an explanation. Payment or publication obstacles require reasons and further handling arrangements, without canceling the confirmed question award.

SingularityX develops adopted questions into `SX-OR-` public challenges, preserves the proposer's contribution record and supplies the link in the [publication records](STATUS.md). Proposers are not required to fund awards or guarantee successful solutions.

**The current program is a call for questions. Solution submissions for a resulting challenge open separately through that challenge’s formal announcement.** Its statement specifies objectives, deliverables, acceptance criteria, awards, slots, budget, review and payment arrangements, and permissions. This page publishes question awards only; it does not activate solution, improvement, verification or major-project awards. Proposers who later solve a challenge must follow that separate announcement.

## 6. Disclosure and rights

Materials are viewed only as necessary for question assessment, reconsideration, adoption records and retention. Question awards recognize the adopted question contribution, together with agreed permission to publish the statement. They do not automatically transfer all intellectual property or grant commercial use of exploratory code, data or full reports.

Question content, summaries, attachments and attribution are confirmed before adoption. Development of the challenge preserves sources and contributions without expanding agreed disclosure. Use of full code, reports or restricted data requires separate terms; third-party and open-source licenses continue to apply.

Names, contact details and payment information are not public by default. Do not submit secrets, account credentials, inside information, customer records or data you cannot disclose. Non-public materials must not be supplied to third-party models or other participants without authorization. Personal-information processing follows the [Privacy Policy](../docs/privacy-policy.md).

## 7. Continuing proposals and reconsideration

Question proposals remain open. Adoption or non-adoption of one question does not affect others. Rule or award changes are announced 3 working days in advance, specifying scope and relative effective timing. Adopted questions retain confirmed amounts without retrospective reductions, additional obligations or expanded permissions. Suspension notices specify scope and treatment of existing submissions.

Send objections concerning adoption, attribution, awards or disclosure to `join@singularityx.tech`, identifying the submission, disputed matters and supporting reasons. Responding within 5 working days of the decision is encouraged without restricting legal rights. The original decision-maker cannot alone issue the final reconsideration; good-faith objections must not reduce confirmed awards.

Truthfully described unsuccessful attempts can evidence investigation; failure to achieve a successful outcome is not itself misconduct. Fabricated reasoning or experiments, plagiarism and misappropriated contributions require evidence review and an opportunity to respond before action under the applicable rules.
