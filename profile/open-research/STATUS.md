## Adoption and publication records

No adopted questions or resulting challenges have yet been announced under this program. After formal adoption, the platform pays the confirmed question award and develops the question into an `SX-OR-` public challenge. This page will provide authorized contribution information, question summaries, award status and formal challenge links.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Under question review | Research value, prior thinking or investigation and remaining difficulties are assessed; no formal adoption decision yet |
| Adopted, awaiting challenge publication | A formal adoption notice and award confirmation have been issued; payment and publication proceed under the rules |
| Public challenge published | A formal challenge is available, with solution requirements, awards and open status at its task link |

Formal adoption and the award are confirmed in writing; a participant-created Issue is not adoption. Question awards are paid independently of a successful solution. Changes to publication arrangements do not cancel confirmed awards. Non-adopted proposals may be supplemented with further thinking or investigation and resubmitted.

## Challenge participation

This page currently supports question proposals and subsequent publication records. It does not open standalone award applications for solutions, improvements or verification work. Challenges developed from adopted questions separately publish submission requirements, actual awards, slots, budgets and acceptance criteria.

Public records contain only authorized attribution and materials, excluding private contact and payment details. Corrections to question and award records are retained for participants to consult.
