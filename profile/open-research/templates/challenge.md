# Public Challenge Template for an Adopted Question

[中文](challenge.zh-CN.md) | [Question program rules](../README.md)

SingularityX uses this template to develop an adopted question into a public challenge. It is not an open task or a route for a proposer to submit completed work. The current question call accepts unresolved questions with prior thinking or investigation only. Before opening solutions, the platform completes the fields below and arranges the relevant budget and permissions.

> **All rights of interpretation for this challenge and its award rules are reserved by SingularityX Evolution (零界演化).** These rights are exercised within applicable law and principles of fairness and good faith, without altering established reward commitments or excluding participants' legal rights.
